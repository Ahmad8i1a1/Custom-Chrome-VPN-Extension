🚀 Pro Tip: Bypassing Regional DPI Firewalls (Jump Jump Style)

In regions with strict DPI firewalls (like Pakistan, UAE, or China), standard unencrypted SOCKS5 ports (1080/8080) are often dropped by ISPs. For 100% reliable connection:

  • Always select Port 443 (HTTPS) or Smart Auto. Standard HTTPS over port 443 cannot be blocked because it blends into ordinary encrypted web traffic.
  • You can deploy a 100% free Cloudflare Worker (100,000 requests/day forever) and add it here as your personal encrypted tunnel.
  • If you run tools like Clash, v2rayN, Shadowsocks, or Jump Jump, enter 127.0.0.1 and your local socks port (e.g. 10808 or 7890) above to tunnel Chrome directly through your high-speed core!
